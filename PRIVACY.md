Privacy Policy for ScrollFrame PDF

Last updated: November 21, 2026

This policy describes how ScrollFrame PDF handles information when you use the Chrome extension.

Information handled

When you scan or capture a page, the extension processes webpage structure and layout information to find scrolling sections and embedded frames.

When you create a capture, the extension saves screenshot images and associated information, including the source page title, page URL, relevant embedded-frame URLs, capture time, and section details. It also saves your capture preferences.

Captured content may contain personal or sensitive information depending on the page and sections you choose. The extension does not separately extract or classify those details.

During selection and capture, the extension temporarily handles selection events, cancellation controls, and scroll positions to operate the picker, capture content, and restore the page. It does not keep a behavioral interaction log.

How information is used

Information is used to provide the requested capture, display progress, generate previews and PDFs, reopen saved captures, and restore temporary changes made during capture.

The extension does not use captured information for advertising, profiling, creditworthiness, lending, or purposes unrelated to its capture and PDF features.

Local processing and storage

Screenshot processing and PDF generation take place in your browser.

Captures and associated information are saved in the extension's local browser storage. Preferences are also stored locally. Temporary capture status is held in browser session storage.

The extension does not upload captured information to the developer or an external service. It does not include analytics or advertising services, and no account is required.

Retention and deletion

Saved captures remain locally until you delete them through the extension, remove the extension, or clear its stored data. You can delete individual captures or all saved captures through the results viewer.

Downloaded PDFs remain in the location where you save them. Deleting a capture from the extension does not delete an exported PDF.

Permissions

The extension uses access to the selected tab, script injection, storage, frame information, and website access to identify and capture scrolling content. Embedded frames may require access to domains different from the main page. Local file access is subject to Chrome's separate user-controlled setting.

Limited use

Information accessed through Chrome APIs is used only to provide the capture and PDF features, in accordance with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy and its Limited Use requirements.

Sharing

The extension does not sell captured information or transmit it to third parties. You control whether you share an exported PDF and with whom.

Support

For questions or concerns, use the support option provided on the ScrollFrame PDF Chrome Web Store listing. If you voluntarily include personal information in a support request, that information is handled through the support service and used to respond to your request.

Changes

This policy will be updated if the extension's data practices change.
