SCROLLFRAME PDF — QUICK START

1. Extract the ZIP. Keep the scrollframe-pdf folder somewhere permanent.
2. Open chrome://extensions in Chrome.
3. Turn on Developer mode.
4. Choose Load unpacked and select the scrollframe-pdf folder
   (the folder with manifest.json inside).
5. Pin ScrollFrame PDF from Chrome's extensions menu.

TO CAPTURE YOUR TASK CONTENT

Open your task page, click the extension, choose Pick a scrolling section,
and click inside the content panel. Keep that tab active while it scrolls.
When the review page opens, click Download PDF.

To include every detected scrolling panel, choose Capture all sections.
The result is one PDF with the main page and each section in order.
You can also use Scan page to choose several specific sections.

If content appears slowly, choose a slower Wait after scrolling setting.
Use the Stop button or Escape to stop and keep the part already captured.

Everything runs locally. There is no account, subscription, or build step.
Chrome's website-access permission is used to inspect embedded frames.
See README.md for details and limitations.
